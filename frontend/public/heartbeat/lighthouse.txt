The Lighthouse at Port Almira

Port Almira is a small fishing town on a rocky stretch of coast. Its lighthouse
stands on the northern breakwater and was first lit in 1883, after two trading
ships were lost on the reef in a single winter.

The current keeper is Marisol Ferrandiz, who took over from her uncle and has
tended the lamp for nineteen years. She keeps a logbook of every passing ship
and repaints the tower white and red each spring.

Visitors may climb the 112 steps on Sundays between April and October.
