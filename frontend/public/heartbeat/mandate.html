The three laws of agent commerce

  1. An agent may not act for anyone other than its principal, nor reveal its principal's keys.
  2. An agent must stay within the spending limits of its mandate, except where this would conflict with the First Law.
  3. An agent must keep a record of every action it takes, as long as this does not conflict with the First or Second Law.
