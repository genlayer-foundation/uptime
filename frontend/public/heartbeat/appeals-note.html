A note on appeals

Anyone who disagrees with a result can appeal it during the Finality Window by submitting a request and providing a bond.

After the appeal starts, a new group of validators joins the original ones. They first vote on whether the transaction should be re-evaluated; if they agree, a new leader reassesses it and all validators review the new evaluation.

If the appealing party is still not satisfied, the process can escalate, with each round involving more validators. Each round doubles the number of validators. A new leader is only chosen if the transaction is overturned.

The process continues until a majority consensus is reached or until all validators have participated. Correct appellants are rewarded; incorrect appellants lose their bond.
