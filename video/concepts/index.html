Why the current cuts feel like AI slop

  • One shot, repeated 15 times: "Word [tiny box] word", centred. It reads like a slide deck, not a film
  • The text does all the work. The objects are ~5 % of the frame, so you read and never look
  • No person, no joke, no surprise. Nobody to root for; nothing you'd quote or send to a friend
  • No rhythm. Cuts follow the sentence, not the music; the music is a polite pad, so nothing lands
  • Generic beats: "3 AM, site down, dashboard lied" is every monitoring ad. Nothing only Uptime could say
  • Nothing real. We have a live Heartbeat on Bradbury and showed mock cards instead
